Paper: PMC12345913.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal activity observed at 37 °C.
- Stable activity observed across a broad temperature range (20–50 °C).
- Decreased activity noted after 1 hour at 60–70 °C.
- Complete inactivation observed after 1 hour at 80 °C.
- Demonstrated exceptional cold tolerance, achieving significant bactericidal activity at 4 °C.
- The phage exhibited robust functionality across a broad temperature range, supporting its use in cold chain applications.

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from sewage samples.
- The phage demonstrated stability across a wide pH range (4–11), indicating environmental robustness.

Concise Summary:
The bacteriophage exhibits optimal lytic activity at 37 °C, but demonstrates superior and highly relevant bactericidal efficacy at low temperatures, specifically achieving a 99.9% reduction of the target pathogen in salmon within 6 hours at 4 °C. The phage displays remarkable environmental tolerance, maintaining stability across a broad pH range (4–11) and a wide temperature spectrum (20–50 °C). While activity decreases significantly at temperatures $\ge 60$ °C, the cold tolerance is a key feature supporting its application in the refrigerated seafood cold chain. The phage was isolated from sewage and shows general environmental robustness, making it suitable for use in food preservation under chilled storage conditions.
