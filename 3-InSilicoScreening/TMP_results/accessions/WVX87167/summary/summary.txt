Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal growth temperature: 37 °C (for bacterial growth/infection studies).

- Environmental source/habitat: Environmental water sample (Thasala, Thailand).
- Adaptation notes: No explicit classification (e.g., thermophilic, psychrophilic) was given. The phage demonstrated stability across a range of temperatures, suggesting mesophilic tolerance, but the data points to a clear upper limit of viability.

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from an environmental water sample.

Concise Summary:
The phage exhibited optimal activity and stability at physiological temperatures, specifically showing stability up to 50 °C, while bacterial growth and lytic activity were assessed at 37 °C. The phage maintained viability across a broad range, from 4 °C to 50 °C, but showed marked thermal instability above this range, losing viability at 80 °C. The phage was isolated from an environmental water source. The study details its robust performance under standard laboratory incubation conditions (37 °C) for both bacterial growth and phage activity, indicating mesophilic characteristics, without classifying it as thermophilic or psychrophilic.
