Paper: PMC13201067.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal growth temperature: 37°C
- Growth temperature range: Not explicitly stated, but growth was conducted at 37°C.
- Survival temperature range: CueP showed stability between 35°C and 90°C when copper was present. In the absence of copper, thermal destabilization occurred above 50°C.
- Incubation conditions: Aerobic incubation at 37°C.
- Environmental source/habitat: Intracellular/phagosomal compartment within host cells (host immune system).
- Mention of adaptations: The protein under study exhibits metal-dependent structural fortification, suggesting adaptation to harsh, fluctuating environments.

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Intracellular/phagosomal compartment within host cells.

Concise Summary:
The organism was studied under laboratory conditions simulating an intracellular/phagosomal environment. Optimal growth and incubation were maintained at 37°C under aerobic conditions. The protein of interest (CueP) demonstrated significant thermal stability, remaining stable between 35°C and 90°C when copper was present. Crucially, the stability of this protein is metal-dependent; in the absence of copper, it undergoes thermal destabilization above 50°C. This suggests a mechanism of thermal resilience linked directly to copper binding, which is vital for survival in the harsh, fluctuating microenvironment encountered within host cells, such as the phagosome, where the organism faces combined copper and oxidative stress.
