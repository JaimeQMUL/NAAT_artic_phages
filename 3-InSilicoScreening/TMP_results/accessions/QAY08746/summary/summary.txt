Environmental Context:
- No environmental source or habitat was mentioned.

Concise Summary:
Sporulation of several species of *Streptomyces* was observed in submerged cultures following nutritional downshift, specifically triggered by phosphate depletion, which was induced by $	ext{Ca}^{2+}$ addition. Spores formed in submerged cultures were reported to be viable and as thermoresistant as aerial spores. The thermoresistance of spore-shaped bodies formed under $	ext{Ca}^{2+}$ and proline supplementation differed from that of aerial spores. No data regarding optimal growth temperature, temperature ranges, or thermal adaptations were presented.
