Concise Summary:
The phage was isolated from hospital sewage water and was characterized using standard laboratory conditions, with optimal growth and activity observed at 37 °C. The phage demonstrated good cold tolerance, remaining stable at 4 °C, and was found to be stable for long-term storage at this temperature. Thermal stability testing showed that while the phage maintained its titer at 4 °C, its infectivity significantly decreased at higher temperatures, losing titer below detection levels at 60 °C and 80 °C. The phage's performance profile suggests mesophilic characteristics, suitable for use under standard physiological temperatures, while exhibiting notable stability in cold storage conditions.
