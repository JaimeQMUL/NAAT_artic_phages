The phage was isolated from sewage and tested against clinical isolates from various bodily fluids (wounds, urine, pus), indicating its relevance in mammalian/animal-associated infections. The phage's stability profile suggests suitability for short-term physiological applications but requires consideration for long-term storage and environmental stress.
