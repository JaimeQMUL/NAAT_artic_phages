Environmental Context:

Concise Summary:
This study investigates the auxiliary metabolic gene *pebS* found in T4-like cyanophages infecting marine picocyanobacteria. The research compares the function of *pebS* (encoding a phycoerythrobilin synthase) to the native two-step reduction pathway found in the host cyanobacteria. Phylogenetic and genomic context analyses strongly suggest that *pebS* is phage-derived and absent from the host cyanobacterial genomes. No information regarding the thermal physiology, growth temperature, or adaptation of the organisms was presented.
