Paper: PMC11559063.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal growth temperature: Not explicitly stated for all strains, but growth rates were measured across a range of temperatures.
- Growth temperature range: Growth rates were measured at 15°C, 16°C, 18°C, 20°C, 22°C, 24°C, 26°C, 27°C, 28°C, and 29°C.
- Survival temperature range: The data suggest survival/growth potential across the tested range, with specific strains showing growth up to 29°C (for one strain) and down to 15°C.
- Incubation conditions: Growth experiments were conducted on a 14:10 h light:dark cycle.
- Psychrophilic adaptation: The novel isolates (LLVII and LLVIII grades) were found to be adapted to colder conditions, showing growth rates distinct from the high-light adapted strain, particularly at lower temperatures.
- Cold tolerance: The LL-adapted strains demonstrated growth at lower temperatures compared to the HL-adapted strain.
- Mesophilic growth: The general growth conditions (22–25°C) suggest mesophilic growth for the majority of the tested conditions.
- Thermophilic adaptation: No evidence of thermophilic adaptation was reported.
- Heat tolerance: The HL-adapted strain showed the highest growth rates at higher temperatures (up to 29°C), while the LL-adapted strains showed significantly slower growth at temperatures $\ge 24^{\circ}C$.

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Isolated from the lower euphotic zone in the subtropical North Pacific Ocean (Station ALOHA).
- Extreme Environment: The isolates were obtained from depths typically experiencing very low irradiance levels ($<5 \mu\text{mol photons m}^{-2} \text{s}^{-1}$), indicating adaptation to oligotrophic, deep-water conditions.

Concise Summary:
The novel isolates were characterized physiologically across temperature gradients. Growth rates were measured from $15^{\circ}\text{C}$ to $29^{\circ}\text{C}$. The LL-adapted lineages (LLVII and LLVIII grades) exhibited growth patterns distinct from the HL-adapted lineage, suggesting adaptation to colder, deeper water column conditions. While the HL-adapted strain showed optimal growth at higher temperatures ($\ge 24^{\circ}\text{C}$), the LL-adapted strains maintained measurable growth rates at lower temperatures, indicating psychrotolerance relative to the HL-type. The general growth conditions suggest mesophilic adaptation, with the LL-adapted strains showing a clear physiological niche favoring cooler, low-irradiance environments, consistent with their deep-water origin.
