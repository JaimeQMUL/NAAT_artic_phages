Concise Summary:
The paper details the fabrication and application of periodically asymmetric, molecular-level superlattice-like membranes ($\text{PDDA/Ti}_{0.87}\text{O}_2$ SLMs) for selective ion transport and osmotic power generation using industrial iodine wastewater. The methodology involves layer-by-layer assembly of inorganic nanosheets and polymer layers to create continuous, unobstructed ionic flow channels. The material exhibits high $\text{I}^- / \text{SO}_4^{2-}$ selectivity ($\sim 680$) and facilitates sustainable osmotic power generation for at least 160 hours. The resulting iodine solution also demonstrates high bactericidal efficacy against *E. coli* (99.5% within 30 min). Crucially, the manuscript provides no data regarding the thermal physiology, growth conditions, or temperature adaptations of any associated microorganisms.
