Paper: 42170637.txt

Environmental Context:

Concise Summary:
The study investigated the antibacterial and antibiofilm potential of saponins extracted from the aerial parts of a plant species against multidrug-resistant Gram-negative bacteria. The research focused on the chemical properties and bioactivity of the isolated compounds, showing modest antibacterial activity (MIC of 4 mg/mL) and strong antibiofilm potential (up to 75% inhibition by compound 3). Mechanistically, the compounds were found to upregulate efflux pump genes, and molecular docking confirmed the interaction of one compound with the adeB efflux pump. No information regarding the optimal, range, or adaptation to temperature extremes was presented.
