Concise Summary:
The phage was isolated from hospital sewage water and was characterized using standard laboratory conditions, with optimal bacterial growth and phage assays conducted at 37 °C, suggesting mesophilic characteristics. The phage demonstrated good stability when stored at 4 °C, but its viability significantly decreased at elevated temperatures, showing a loss of titer at 60 °C and 80 °C compared to 4 °C. The phage was stable across a pH range of 4 to 10 after 1 hour incubation at 37 °C, but showed reduced titer at pH 3 and pH 12. These thermal characteristics suggest a mesophilic nature with better tolerance to cold storage than to high temperatures.
