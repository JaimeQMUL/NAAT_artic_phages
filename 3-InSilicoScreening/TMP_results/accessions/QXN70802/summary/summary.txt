Paper: 42154063.txt
Thermal Evidence:

- Specific enzyme activities were measured at 50 ℃ for both xylanase and laccase.
- Optimal activity was observed at 50 ℃ for both key enzymes.

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from high-altitude regions (Pangi-Chamba Himalayas).
- The isolates are described as extremophilic.

Concise Summary:
Lignocellulolytic bacteria were isolated from high-altitude, extremophilic niches. The enzymes produced (laccase and xylanase) exhibit remarkable thermal stability, showing measurable activity across a broad range of 4-90 ℃. Optimal activity for both enzymes was determined at 50 ℃. The overall data suggests robust thermal adaptation, making these isolates candidates for high-temperature industrial applications.
