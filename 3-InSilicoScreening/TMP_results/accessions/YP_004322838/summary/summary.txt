    - Cold tolerance: GB's role in maintaining membrane integrity is noted in the context of stress tolerance.
- No specific enzymes adapted to temperature extremes were detailed, though the general discussion covers stress responses.

Environmental Context:
- The organisms discussed are associated with diverse environments, including soil, and the context implies adaptation to varying environmental conditions, including those that induce heat or cold stress.

Concise Summary:
Glycine betaine (GB) is crucial for enhancing the resistance of both plants and microorganisms to various abiotic stresses, including heat and cold. At high temperatures, GB helps maintain cell membrane fluidity and prevents protein misfolding by acting as a molecular chaperone. Similarly, GB contributes to cold tolerance by helping to maintain membrane integrity. The review emphasizes that the ability to cope with thermal stress is a key aspect of sustainable agriculture, linking GB's protective role to the overall resilience of microbial fertilizers and crops.
