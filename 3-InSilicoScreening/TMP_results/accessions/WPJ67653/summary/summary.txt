Two bacteriophages were isolated from water and wastewater samples collected in Abidjan, Côte d’Ivoire. The phages were propagated by incubating filtered water samples with the host bacterium in Luria-Bertani (LB) medium at 37°C for 48 hours. The study details genome sequencing and functional annotation of these phages, but provides no specific data regarding optimal, growth, or survival temperature ranges, nor any evidence of thermophilic, psychrophilic, or general thermal adaptation mechanisms.
