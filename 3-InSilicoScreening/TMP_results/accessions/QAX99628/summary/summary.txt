Paper: PMC12771599.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:

- High stability maintained across a temperature range of 30°C to 60°C for phage viability.
- Significant loss of viability observed at 70°C and 80°C for the phage.

- The endolysin's stability was assessed using Differential Scanning Fluorimetry (DSF), which measures the melting temperature (T m).

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from river water (Ganga river water).

Concise Summary:
The phage demonstrated robust stability at temperatures ranging from 30°C to 60°C, with optimal stability noted at 30°C. The phage showed significant loss of viability at 70°C and 80°C. The endolysin exhibited optimal structural stability at pH 6.0, with a determined melting temperature (T m) of 54.5°C. The endolysin's stability was confirmed to be unaffected by the presence of various divalent metal ions (Mn²⁺, Ca²⁺, Co²⁺, Mg²⁺, Cu²⁺) within the tested concentration range. The organism was isolated from river water. The data suggest favorable thermal resilience for both the phage and its endolysin, with the endolysin's stability profile being key for formulation development.
