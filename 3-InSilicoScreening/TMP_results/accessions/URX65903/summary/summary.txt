Paper: 42176583.txt
Thermal Evidence:

- Heat stimulation (Hs) and Microwave stimulation (Ws) were used as treatments, indicating thermal application was a key variable.

Environmental Context:
- The larvae were reared on chicken manure, which serves as the environmental source/habitat.

Concise Summary:
The study investigated the enhancement of antibacterial efficacy in larvae reared on chicken manure when exposed to enteric pathogens. Thermal treatments, specifically heat stimulation (Hs) and microwave stimulation (Ws), were applied to the larvae. These stimulation methods were shown to significantly upregulate the expression of antimicrobial peptides (defensin and cecropin) and enhance antibacterial activity against *S. pullorum*. The optimal retention of anti-*S. pullorum* activity was achieved after drying the larvae following pretreatment with a starch-sucrose-Tween-80 solution, with the best result observed in the microwave-stimulated (Ws) group. The research provides technical guidance for maximizing antibacterial protein production via controlled thermal stimulation protocols.
