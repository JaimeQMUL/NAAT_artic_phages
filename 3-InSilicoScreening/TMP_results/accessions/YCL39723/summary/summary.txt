Concise Summary:
The study utilized wastewater-derived bacteriophages isolated from WWTPs. The bacterial hosts were maintained under mesophilic conditions (optimal growth at 37 °C). The primary focus regarding temperature was the stability of the phages, where encapsulation provided significant thermal protection. Encapsulated phages retained detectable viability at elevated temperatures (up to 80 °C for 10 min) and demonstrated robust stability during long-term storage at both 4 °C and 21 °C over three months. The phages showed good cold tolerance, maintaining high titers at 4 °C. The protective effect of the alginate matrix was crucial in mitigating thermal inactivation, suggesting enhanced heat tolerance for the formulated product compared to the free phage particles.
