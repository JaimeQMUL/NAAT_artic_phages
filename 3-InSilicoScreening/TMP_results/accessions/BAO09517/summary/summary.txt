- No mention of thermophilic, psychrophilic, mesophilic, heat tolerance, cold tolerance, or temperature-adapted enzymes was found.

Environmental Context:
- The viruses were studied in the context of the dsDNA virosphere, with sources including *Bacillus* species, *Staphylococcus* species, *Listeria* species, *Enterococcus* species, and *Lactobacillus* species.

Concise Summary:
This study focuses on the systematic reclassification of tailed bacteriophages, specifically proposing the establishment of a new family, Herelleviridae, to accommodate the genetic diversity of SPO1-related viruses previously grouped in Spounavirinae. The analysis utilized comparative genomics, core genome analysis, and marker gene phylogenetics across 93 analyzed genomes. The classification methods employed were diverse, ranging from high-throughput clustering (vConTACT, GRAViTy) to whole-genome comparisons (VICTOR, DICE) and proteomic analyses (Phage Proteomic Tree). The authors emphasize that the classification is based on genetic relatedness rather than morphology, advocating for a shift from historical, morphology-based taxonomy to a phylogenomic approach. The proposed taxonomy establishes five subfamilies within Herelleviridae: Spounavirinae, Bastillevirinae, Brockvirinae, Twortvirinae, and Jasinskavirinae. The study concludes that the convergence of results across multiple, complementary methods supports this major taxonomic revision of the Caudovirales order, aiming to create a system that reflects true genetic relationships across the prokaryotic virosphere.
