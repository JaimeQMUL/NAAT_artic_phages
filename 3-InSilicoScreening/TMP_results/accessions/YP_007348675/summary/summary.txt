Concise Summary:
The phage-derived endolysin demonstrated robust thermal stability, retaining activity after incubation at 50°C, 60°C, and 70°C. Its stability profile indicates resilience up to 80°C (10% loss after 15 min) and 90°C (50% loss after 60 min), while complete inactivation occurred at 110°C. The enzyme maintained activity over a month at 4°C. Bacterial assays were conducted at 37°C, and the enzyme showed stability across a wide pH range (pH 2.6 to 10). The phage was isolated from environmental sewage samples, and the enzyme's demonstrated thermostability suggests potential for use under varied thermal conditions.
