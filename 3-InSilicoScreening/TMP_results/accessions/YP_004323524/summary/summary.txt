The organism is a polyextremophilic, thermophilic, halotolerant cyanobacterium originally isolated from thermal springs. It exhibits robust growth capabilities, with documented survival up to 50 °C. The study investigated its lipid metabolism in response to high temperature (40 °C). Under elevated temperatures, the organism undergoes significant membrane lipid remodeling, characterized by a shift in acyl unsaturation from unsaturated to saturated fatty acids (e.g., 16:1 to 16:0) to maintain membrane stability. This adaptation is paralleled by changes in nonpolar lipid accumulation, such as increased saturated TAG species. These findings highlight the organism's capacity for thermal adaptation through controlled lipidomic restructuring, which is crucial for its survival in extreme thermal environments.
