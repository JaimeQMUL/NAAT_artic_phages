Concise Summary:
The research details a novel class of contractile injection system (CIS) effectors, related to phage tape measure proteins, found widely within the Actinobacteria. These effectors are packaged within phage-derived nanoparticles and translocated to lipid membranes. Functionally, these effectors modify the physiological properties of the host organism, enhancing its adaptation to microbial competition. The effectors exhibit structural and functional diversity, with one example forming a novel toxin-antitoxin system. The overall significance lies in demonstrating that phage elements can act as reconfigurable platforms for bacterial adaptation to environmental conditions, suggesting a role in tuning bacterial phenotypes within communities.
