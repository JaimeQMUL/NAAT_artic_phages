- Cold Tolerance: Stable at 4 °C.
- Enzymes/Adaptations: No specific mention of temperature-adapted enzymes or membrane adaptations was provided.

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Hospital sewage water sample.

Concise Summary:
The phage was isolated from hospital sewage water and was characterized using standard laboratory conditions, with optimal growth and activity observed at 37 °C. The phage demonstrated good cold tolerance, remaining stable at 4 °C, and was found to be stable for long-term storage at this temperature. Thermal stability testing showed that while the phage maintained its titer at 4 °C, its infectivity significantly decreased at higher temperatures, losing titer below detection levels at 60 °C and 80 °C. The phage's performance suggests mesophilic characteristics, as its optimal activity was measured at 37 °C, and no evidence of thermophilic or psychrophilic adaptation was reported. The phage was confirmed to be strictly lytic, and its stability profile is crucial for its potential application as a biocontrol agent.
