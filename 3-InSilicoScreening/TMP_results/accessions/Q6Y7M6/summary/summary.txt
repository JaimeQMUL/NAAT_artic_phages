- No environmental source or habitat was mentioned for any microorganism.

Concise Summary:
This study evaluates the use of an oxidized guar gum-based hydrogel crosslinked with gelatin (OxGG:Gel) in a rat model of sepsis induced by cecal ligation and puncture (CLP). The analysis focuses on the hydrogel's protective effects on physiological parameters in septic rats. Treatment with the 2% solution administered intraperitoneally (20 mg/kg) reduced signs of prostration and piloerection and showed a protective effect on temperature and glycemia compared to the untreated CLP group. Conversely, the per oral administration of a 5% solution (50 mg/kg) was associated with adverse effects (e.g., respiratory distress) due to potential airway obstruction from the viscous gel, irrespective of the sepsis status. The study concludes that the administration route and concentration significantly modulate the therapeutic efficacy of the hydrogel for sepsis management. No microbiological data regarding temperature requirements or environmental adaptation were presented.
