This study focuses on developing a controlled-release antimicrobial food packaging system using a citronellal-based coating and a PCL barrier on PLA. The research details the modulation of antimicrobial agent release rates (e.g., 30 nm PCL resulted in significantly lower residual release compared to no barrier) to sustain antimicrobial activity, achieving log reductions of up to 3.97 for *L. monocytogenes* and 3.37 for LAB with the 30 nm PCL layer.
