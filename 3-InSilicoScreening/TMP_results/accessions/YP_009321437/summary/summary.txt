Concise Summary:
The study investigated the interaction of phototropism and gravitropism in a cyanobacterium. The physiological mechanisms observed relate to the directional sensing of light, adhesion, and external physical forces (including gravity). Pigment distribution (phycobilins and chlorophyll) is regulated by these directional cues, with polyphosphate bodies being key regulators. The paper reports the first known intracellular directional gravity response mechanism in a prokaryote, but provides no data regarding the organism's thermal optimum, growth range, or adaptation to temperature extremes.
