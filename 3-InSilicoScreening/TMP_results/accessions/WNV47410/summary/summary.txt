- Enzymes adapted to temperature: No specific enzymes adapted to temperature were detailed.
- Membrane adaptations: Not mentioned.
- Thermal stress responses: The phage demonstrated stability across a range of temperatures, with significant loss of titer observed at extreme temperatures.

Concise Summary:
The bacteriophage was isolated from hospital sewage water. Its optimal growth and assay temperature was 37 °C. The phage demonstrated good cold tolerance, maintaining titer at 4 °C and showing long-term stability over 5 years at this temperature. The stability profile indicates resilience in the cold range compared to elevated temperatures.
