Paper: 42176583.txt

Concise Summary:
The study investigated the enhancement of antibacterial efficacy in larvae reared on chicken manure when exposed to enteric pathogens. Thermal treatments, specifically heat stimulation (Hs) and microwave stimulation (Ws), were applied to the larvae. These stimulation methods were shown to significantly upregulate the expression of antimicrobial peptides (defensin and cecropin) and enhance antibacterial activity against *S. pullorum*. Furthermore, a specific drying pretreatment (1% starch-5% sucrose-0.1% Tween-80) was found to optimally retain anti-*S. pullorum* activity following desiccation. The research provides technical guidance related to optimizing antibacterial protein production via thermal and desiccation treatments.
