    - Cold tolerance: GB's role in maintaining membrane integrity is noted in the context of stress tolerance.

Concise Summary:
Glycine betaine (GB) is a crucial compatible solute that enhances the resistance of both plants and microorganisms to various abiotic stresses, including high temperature and cold. At high temperatures, GB helps maintain cell membrane fluidity and prevents protein misfolding by acting as a molecular chaperone, thereby stabilizing cellular functions and energy metabolism. Similarly, GB contributes to cold tolerance by helping to maintain membrane integrity. The research heavily focuses on the genetic engineering of GB synthesis pathways in microorganisms, particularly those isolated from extreme environments (e.g., halophiles), to enhance their survival and function under adverse conditions, which inherently includes thermal stress management. The overall theme is leveraging microbial metabolic engineering to create robust, stress-resistant biocontrol agents for sustainable agriculture.
