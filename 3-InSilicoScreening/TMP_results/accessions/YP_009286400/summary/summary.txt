Concise Summary:

Its thermal stability was tested across various temperatures, showing retained activity between 30°C and 50°C for both 30 and 60 minutes, with complete inactivation noted at 80°C. When applied to eggs, the phage maintained efficacy at both room temperature (25°C) and refrigerated temperature (4°C) over 24 hours. The phage was isolated from poultry fecal samples, and its stability profile suggests mesophilic characteristics with good tolerance up to 50°C.
