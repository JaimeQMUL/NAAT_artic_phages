Environmental Context:
- Habitat/source: Intracellular pathogen niche (phagosome) within host immune cells.

Concise Summary:
The organism is studied in the context of its intracellular lifestyle within host phagosomes, which presents a hostile environment characterized by oxidative stress and metal toxicity. Optimal growth and experimental incubation conditions were maintained at 37°C under aerobic conditions. The protein of interest, CueP, demonstrates a critical dependence on copper for structural integrity; in the presence of copper, it remains stable across a broad range (35°C to 90°C), whereas its stability rapidly decreases above 50°C when copper is absent. This metal-dependent thermal stability suggests a mechanism for maintaining protein function in fluctuating, stressful host environments, indicating adaptation to challenging, non-optimal conditions rather than classification as a specific thermophile or psychrophile.


Paper: 42206853.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Growth temperature range: 22°C (elevated growth observed in water-based sauces over 24 h).
- Survival temperature range: 4°C (stable survival observed for two weeks in both water- and oil-based sauces).
- Incubation conditions: Storage at 22°C and 4°C.
- Mention of adaptation: None explicitly mentioned.

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Food matrices (tahini sauces).

Concise Summary:
Bacterial survival was assessed across storage temperatures of 22°C and 4°C. At 22°C, water-based sauces showed significant growth (6 logs increase over 24 h), while oil-based sauces remained stable. At 4°C, survival was maintained for two weeks in both water- and oil-based sauces. Furthermore, storage in lemon juice-based sauces resulted in no recovery of bacteria after one week at 4°C. The study highlights the inhibitory effect of low pH (pH 3.6) and refrigeration on bacterial viability.
