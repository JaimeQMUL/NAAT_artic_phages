Paper: PMC10253784.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Stable up to 65 °C.
- Inactivated by heating at 75 °C (15 min) and 100 °C (30 min).
- Significant decrease in viability observed when exposed to temperatures between 25 °C and 65 °C for 15 or 30 minutes.
- Most stable temperature for storage was determined to be 4 °C compared to -20 °C and 25 °C after 5 months.

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from pigeon feces in the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

Concise Summary:
The bacteriophage was found to be stable up to 65 °C, showing inactivation at 75 °C (15 min) and 100 °C (30 min). Viability significantly decreased when exposed to temperatures between 25 °C and 65 °C. The most stable storage temperature was determined to be 4 °C. The phage was isolated from pigeon feces in the UAE. The authors did not provide an explicit classification (e.g., mesophile, psychrotolerant) but detailed its thermal stability profile for potential use in food processing and preservation.
