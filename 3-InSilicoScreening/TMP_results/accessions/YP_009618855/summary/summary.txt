Paper: PMC13153029.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal stability range: 4 °C to 50 °C.
- Stability decline observed after 80 min at 60 °C, with an 87.8% reduction in titer.
- Rapid decline at 70 °C.
- Stable titer maintained after 1 year of storage at 4 °C.
- The phage maintained stability within the range of 4 °C−50 °C.

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from untreated sewage at a hospital.

Concise Summary:
The phage exhibits robust thermal stability, maintaining activity across a broad range from 4 °C to 50 °C. Long-term storage stability was confirmed at 4 °C for over a year. Thermal inactivation was noted at higher temperatures, with significant titer reduction observed after 80 minutes at 60 °C and rapid loss of activity at 70 °C. The phage was isolated from untreated sewage, suggesting adaptation to environmental conditions, but the authors did not explicitly classify it as thermophilic, psychrophilic, or mesophilic; however, the stability profile suggests mesophilic suitability with good cold tolerance and moderate heat tolerance up to 50 °C.
