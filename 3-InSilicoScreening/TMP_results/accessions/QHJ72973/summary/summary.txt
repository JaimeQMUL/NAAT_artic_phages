The isolated phages demonstrated optimal stability and viability at physiological temperatures (25–37°C) for extended periods (up to 180 min). The phages were isolated from municipal wastewater canals, suggesting adaptation to a complex, potentially fluctuating environmental niche, though no specific thermophilic or psychrophilic adaptations were explicitly classified by the authors based on the tested ranges.
