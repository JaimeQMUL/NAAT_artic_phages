Environmental Context:
- UvsXt: Isolated from phages colonizing Icelandic hot springs and geysers, with a temperature range of 62–88°C. Classified as thermophilic.

- General: Both enzymes were isolated from extreme environments (hot springs and deep sea).

Concise Summary:
Two novel phage recombinases, UvsXt and UvsXp, were characterized. UvsXt, sourced from hot springs (62–88°C), exhibits superior thermostability, with a measured Tm up to 71.4°C under stabilizing conditions, classifying it as thermophilic. UvsXp, sourced from deep Arctic seawater (0.4°C), displays psychrophilic characteristics, with a lower but stable Tm. Both enzymes retain structural homology to known recombinases. Functionally, UvsXt demonstrated enhanced utility in RT-LAMP assays at 65°C, a temperature where the control enzyme (EcRecA) is denatured. The differential stability profiles—high temperature robustness for UvsXt versus broad chemical stability for UvsXp—suggest distinct biotechnological applications for each enzyme.
