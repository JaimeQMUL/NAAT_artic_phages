Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Isolated from the gut of *Periplaneta americana* L. (an insect).
- Classification: No explicit classification (e.g., thermophilic, psychrophilic) was given by the authors, but the optimal growth conditions suggest mesophilic adaptation, particularly around 37°C.

Concise Summary:
The bacteriophage was characterized with an optimal culture temperature of 37°C, and the optimal plaque formation time was determined to be 6 hours. Stability analysis revealed that the phage maintained good titer stability across a range including 4°C, 21°C, 37°C, and 50°C. However, significant titer reduction was noted when temperatures exceeded 50°C, with near complete inactivation observed at 70°C. The phage was isolated from the gut of *Periplaneta americana* L. The stability profile suggests mesophilic characteristics, with robust activity maintained up to 50°C, making it suitable for use in environments mimicking mammalian body temperature (37°C) while showing limited tolerance to extreme heat.
