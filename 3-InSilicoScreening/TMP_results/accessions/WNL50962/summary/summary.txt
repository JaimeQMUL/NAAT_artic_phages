- No specific environmental source or habitat was detailed, other than the context of $\text{CO}_2$ fixation relevant to early Earth conditions.

Concise Summary:
This study investigated the structural and redox adaptability of $\text{CO}_2$ fixation machinery (carboxysomes) in a cyanobacterium under varying $\text{CO}_2$ concentrations (0.04% vs. 3%). The research utilized redox sensors and microscopy to track subcellular redox states across different compartmentalization levels (cytosol, procarboxysomes, carboxysomes). The findings demonstrate that the carboxysome structure is highly adaptable to environmental $\text{CO}_2$ levels, forming partially encapsulated, procarboxysome-like structures under high $\text{CO}_2$ (3%) conditions, which exposes contents to the cytosol. No data regarding temperature optima, ranges, or thermal adaptations were presented.
