Environmental Context:
- The phage was isolated from the gut of an insect species (*Periplaneta americana* L.).

Concise Summary:
The phage exhibits optimal growth and stability at 37°C, which was used for plaque assays, host range testing, and one-step growth curve determination. It shows good thermal stability across a range including 4°C, 21°C, 37°C, and 50°C. However, stability significantly declines above 50°C, with near complete inactivation noted at 70°C. The phage was isolated from the gut of an insect species. The analysis suggests mesophilic growth characteristics, with robust activity maintained up to 50°C, indicating good general environmental tolerance within the physiological range of the host pathogen, but lacking thermophilic adaptation.
