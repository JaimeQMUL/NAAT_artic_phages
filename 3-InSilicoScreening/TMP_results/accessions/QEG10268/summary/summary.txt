Concise Summary:

Its activity was noted to decrease significantly at 50°C and was completely inactivated after 1 hour at 70°C. The phage was characterized as having good thermal stability compared to other phages below 40°C. All in vitro characterization, including one-step growth curve determination and stability testing, was conducted at 37°C. The phage was isolated from hospital sewage, indicating an environmental source. No explicit classification (e.g., thermophilic, psychrophilic) was provided by the authors, but the observed stability profile suggests mesophilic characteristics with good resilience across a moderate temperature spectrum.
