Paper: PMC13153029.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal stability range: 4 °C to 50 °C.
- Stability observed at 4 °C, 28 °C, 37 °C, and 50 °C.
- Decline noted after 80 min at 60 °C, with an 87.8% reduction in titer.
- Rapid decline observed at 70 °C.
- Stable titer maintained after 1 year of storage at 4 °C.
- Optimal stability range for pH was 4–10.

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from untreated sewage at a hospital.
- No explicit classification regarding thermophilic, psychrophilic, or mesophilic adaptation was provided; the stability profile suggests mesophilic activity with good cold tolerance and moderate heat tolerance up to 50 °C.

Concise Summary:
The phage exhibits robust stability across a wide temperature range, maintaining activity from 4 °C up to 50 °C. Long-term storage stability was confirmed at 4 °C over one year. While activity significantly declines at 60 °C and 70 °C, the phage demonstrates good tolerance to moderate thermal stress. The optimal pH range is 4–10. The phage was isolated from untreated hospital sewage, suggesting an environmental origin. The stability profile is consistent with a mesophilic organism, showing notable cold tolerance and moderate heat resistance, but no evidence of thermophilic adaptation was reported.
