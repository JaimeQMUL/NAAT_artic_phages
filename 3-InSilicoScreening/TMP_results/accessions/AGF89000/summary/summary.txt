Paper: 42206853.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Growth temperature range: 22°C (elevated growth observed in water-based sauces over 24 h).
- Survival temperature range: 4°C (stable survival observed for two weeks in both water- and oil-based sauces).
- Incubation conditions: Storage at 22°C and 4°C.
- Mention of adaptation: None explicitly mentioned.

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Food matrices (tahini sauces).

Concise Summary:
Bacterial survival was assessed across storage temperatures of 22°C and 4°C. At 22°C, water-based sauces showed significant growth (6 logs increase over 24 h), while oil-based sauces remained stable. At 4°C, both water- and oil-based sauces maintained detectable levels for two weeks. Furthermore, storage in lemon juice-based sauces resulted in the absence of recovery by one week at 4°C. The study highlights the inhibitory effect of low pH (pH 3.6) and refrigeration on bacterial viability.
