Concise Summary:
The phage exhibited optimal activity and bacterial growth conditions at 37 °C. Thermal stability testing revealed that lytic activity was stable between 40 °C and 60 °C, with a gradual decline observed between 60 °C and 80 °C, and complete inactivation at 90 °C. The phage was isolated from medical sewage and demonstrated robust activity within the pH range of 4 to 12, which includes the pH range of seawater (8.0–8.25), suggesting potential for use in marine/seafood applications. The overall evidence points to a mesophilic organism with moderate heat tolerance up to 60 °C.
