Paper: PMC13215166.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- No temperature-related data was provided in the text.

Environmental Context:
- No environmental source or habitat information was provided in the text.

Concise Summary:
This paper provides a comprehensive evaluation of 25 structurally diverse N-nitrosamines using a multi-modal approach combining in vitro (Ames test), in vivo (liver comet, Big Blue transgenic mutation, and duplex sequencing), and in silico methods (quantum mechanical modeling and CYP–binding analysis) to assess genotoxicity and carcinogenicity. The study's primary focus is developing a weight-of-evidence decision tree to derive compound-specific acceptable intakes (AIs) for these impurities, aiming to reduce reliance on animal testing. The analysis highlights that complex API-derived N-nitrosamines are often less mutagenic or have lower potency compared to simple small-molecule dialkyl N-nitrosamines. Key methodological advancements include the superior sensitivity of duplex sequencing (DS) over traditional assays and the integration of computational models (like CADRE QM) with experimental data to propose a risk-based AI derivation framework. The study also correlated physicochemical properties (size, shape, ionization) with predicted and observed mutagenic potential, suggesting that larger, more globular, and neutral compounds tend to be less potent.
