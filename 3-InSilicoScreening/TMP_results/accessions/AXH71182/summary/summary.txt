Concise Summary:
This study focused on the genomic diversity and taxonomic classification of 61 bacteriophages infecting *Bacillus subtilis* group members. The analysis utilized comparative genomics, pangenome analysis, and phylogenetic reconstruction based on various conserved and signature genes (e.g., Major Capsid Protein, poly-gamma-glutamate hydrolase). The research established a correlation between genome size and established phage families (e.g., Salasmaviridae $\rightarrow$ small genomes; Herelleviridae $\rightarrow$ large genomes). Crucially, the study proposed the creation of two new subfamilies based on inter-genera sequence similarities and the identification of unique ortholog signature genes (e.g., MCP Type III for New subfamily1; peptidoglycan binding proteins for New subfamily2). The analysis confirmed that the phages are associated with *B. subtilis* group species, which are relevant in food industries. No direct thermal physiological data regarding growth or survival temperatures was reported for the phages.
