This study focuses on the auxiliary metabolic gene *pebS*, an enzyme involved in phycoerythrobilin biosynthesis, found in cyanophages infecting marine picocyanobacteria. Concise Summary:
The study investigated seasonal and spatial variations of a dominant cyanobacterium in a temperate semi-enclosed bay. Abundance exhibited a strong positive correlation with temperature, peaking in summer (optimal range $27.5–29.5^{\circ}\text{C}$) and reaching minimums in winter, consistent with temperature-driven growth patterns.
