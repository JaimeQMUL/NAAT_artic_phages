Concise Summary:
A novel, broad-host-range lytic phage was isolated from untreated hospital sewage. The phage exhibits robust environmental stability, demonstrating tolerance to temperatures up to 60 ℃. The phage's biological characteristics were analyzed using a clinically relevant bacterial strain as the host. The phage was found to be highly effective against multiple sequence types of a carbapenem-resistant Enterobacter complex. Genomic analysis confirmed the phage is lytic and lacks genes associated with lysogeny or antibiotic resistance, suggesting therapeutic potential. The phage's stability across a wide pH range (2 to 11) further indicates environmental robustness.
