Genome sequences of 19 Klebsiella phages of the genus Przondovirus.

Here, we describe the genomes of 19 Klebsiella phages of the genus Przondovirus. The ranges of
genome lengths, GC content, and numbers of predicted protein-coding genes were 39,014-41,853 bp,
52.4-53.5%, and 53-61, respectively. Based on key parameters, these phages appear to be lytic and
suitable for therapeutic use.
