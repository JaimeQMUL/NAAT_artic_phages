Concise Summary:
Three lytic bacteriophages were isolated from sewage samples originating from livestock farms. The phages demonstrated viability across a temperature range of 25°C to 42°C, with optimal stability observed at 37°C. Viability was significantly reduced at 65°C and completely lost at 80°C. The phages were assessed under controlled incubation conditions simulating environmental and physiological temperatures. The overall profile suggests mesophilic characteristics, with robust survival at ambient and body temperatures, but limited heat tolerance above 42°C.
