Paper: PMC12820288.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal growth temperature: 37 °C (used as control condition for stability and activity assays).
- Growth temperature range: Assays were conducted at 37 °C.
- Survival temperature range: Stable at 25 °C and 40 °C; marked reduction in viability observed at 55, 60, and 65 °C; no viable phages at 70 °C.
- Incubation conditions: Assays were performed at 37 °C for lytic activity and biofilm studies.
- Environmental source/habitat: Isolated from influent sewage samples.
- Thermophilic/Psychrophilic/Mesophilic: The phages exhibited optimal performance at 37 °C, consistent with mesophilic organisms.
- Heat tolerance: Demonstrated reduced viability at temperatures $\ge$ 55 °C.
- Cold tolerance: Stability was assessed at 25 °C, showing no significant reduction in titer compared to the control.

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Influent sewage samples from various hospital and community wastewater sites in Dakar, Senegal.

Concise Summary:
The phages were isolated from influent sewage samples. Their optimal activity and stability were observed at 37 °C, indicating mesophilic characteristics. The phages demonstrated good stability at lower temperatures (25 °C and 40 °C) but showed significant loss of viability when incubated at 55 °C and above, with no viability detected at 70 °C. This thermal profile suggests adaptation to ambient or body-related temperatures rather than extreme thermophilic or psychrophilic niches.
