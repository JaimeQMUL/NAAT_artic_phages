Paper: PMC11312844.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal growth temperature: 37°C (mentioned for multiple assays).
- Growth temperature range: 37°C (standard incubation).
- Survival temperature range: Tested viability at 40°C, 60°C, and 80°C for 30 min; complete inactivation observed at 100°C.
- Incubation conditions: Standard incubation at 37°C for various assays (e.g., 24h for some assays).
- Environmental source/habitat: Sesame oil meal (SOM) from food waste.
- Thermophilic adaptation: No explicit mention.
- Psychrophilic adaptation: No explicit mention.
- Mesophilic growth: Implied by optimal growth at 37°C.
- Heat tolerance: Demonstrated survival at 60°C (retaining >90% viability) and 40°C (minimal decline).
- Cold tolerance: Not explicitly tested or mentioned.
- Enzymes adapted to temperature: Not specified.
- Membrane adaptations: Not specified.
- Thermal stress responses: Demonstrated survival under elevated temperatures up to 80°C.

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Isolated from sesame oil meal (SOM), a food processing residue.

Concise Summary:
The organism exhibits optimal growth at 37°C, consistent with mesophilic characteristics. Thermal stability testing showed viability maintained at 40°C and 60°C (retaining >90% viability after 30 min), but significant decline occurred at 80°C, with complete inactivation observed at 100°C. The organism was isolated from sesame oil meal (SOM), a food waste source. The authors did not classify the organism with specific terms like 'thermophilic' or 'psychrotolerant,' but the observed survival profile indicates robust heat tolerance within the mesophilic range, suggesting potential for industrial processing techniques like spray drying.
