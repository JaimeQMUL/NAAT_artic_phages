Complete genome sequence of Escherichia coli phage vB_EcoP_EcoN5 isolated from Magdalena River, Atlántico, Colombia.

Antibiotic resistance represents a growing threat to public health, requiring alternative therapies.
Here, we report the complete genome sequence and annotation of Escherichia coli bacteriophage
vB_EcoP_EcoN5, isolated from river water in Barranquilla, Colombia. Genomic features suggest
potential applications in future phage therapy strategies.
