While activity significantly declines at 60 °C and 70 °C, the phage demonstrates good tolerance to moderate thermal stress. The optimal pH range is 4–10. The phage was isolated from untreated hospital sewage, suggesting an environmental origin. The stability profile is consistent with a mesophilic organism, showing notable cold tolerance and moderate heat resistance, but no evidence of thermophilic adaptation was reported.
