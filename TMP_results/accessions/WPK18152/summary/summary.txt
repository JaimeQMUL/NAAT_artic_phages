Paper: PMC13153029.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Stable from 4 °C to 50 °C.
- Optimal activity observed at 37 °C (physiological temperature).
- Stability maintained at 4 °C for over a year (long-term storage).
- Stability declined after 80 min at 60 °C, with an 87.8% reduction in titer.
- Rapid decline observed at 70 °C.
- Stable within the temperature range of 4 °C−50 °C.

Environmental Context:
- Isolated from untreated sewage at a hospital.

Concise Summary:
The phage exhibits robust thermal stability, maintaining activity across a broad range from 4 °C to 50 °C. It shows optimal activity at 37 °C, consistent with physiological conditions, and demonstrates excellent long-term storage stability at 4 °C. Thermal inactivation begins to occur significantly above 50 °C, with notable decline observed at 60 °C and rapid loss of titer at 70 °C. The phage was isolated from untreated sewage, suggesting adaptation to a complex, potentially variable environmental matrix, though no explicit classification (e.g., thermophilic, psychrophilic) was provided by the authors.

Paper: PMC12951479.1.txt
Thermal Evidence:
- Optimal growth temperature: 37°C
- Growth temperature range: 30–50°C
- Survival temperature range: Stable within 30–60°C (for one phage); Minimal impact observed between 30–50°C (for the other phage).
- Incubation conditions: 37°C for plaque assessment and initial stability testing.
- Environmental source/habitat: Swine wastewater.

Concise Summary:
The bacteriophages were isolated from swine wastewater. Their optimal growth temperature was determined to be 37°C. The general activity range was noted to be between 30–50°C. Stability testing indicated that one phage maintained relatively stable titers across the 30–60°C range, while the other showed minimal impact between 30–50°C. The phages demonstrated robust tolerance to these moderate temperatures, suggesting mesophilic characteristics. No explicit classification (e.g., thermophilic, psychrophilic) was provided by the authors, but the stability data confirm functionality within a moderate temperature range, which is critical for their application in food preservation (refrigerated and ambient temperatures).

Environmental Context:
- Habitat/Source: Swine wastewater.
